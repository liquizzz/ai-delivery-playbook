# Appendix A: Templates

**Purpose:** the documents sessions fill in. You rarely open them yourself: the session copies the template, asks you what is missing, and fills it in.

**Ask any session:**
> Use the `<template>` template for `<topic>`.

| Template | Written by | Saved as | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Proposal](../../templates/proposal.md) | PO | `docs/product/proposal.md` | Ch. 3, phase 1 |

| [Data notes](../../templates/data-notes.md) | PO + Lead | `docs/product/data-notes.md` | Ch. 3, phase 2 |
| [User story](../../templates/user-story.md) | PO | `docs/product/stories/US-NNN-*.md` | Ch. 3, phase 2 |
